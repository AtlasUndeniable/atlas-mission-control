# Retention Playbook

## The First 30 Days
Retention is decided in the first 30 days. A client who has a scheduled next action at every point in the first month renews at a materially higher rate.

## Check-in Change Requests
When a client asks to change their check-in cadence, treat it as a retention signal, not an admin task. Confirm the change, then book a review at the end of the following cycle.
