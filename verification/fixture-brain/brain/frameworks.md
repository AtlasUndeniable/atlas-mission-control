# Undeniable Frameworks

## The Onboarding Spine
Every new coaching client moves through four gates: intake call, baseline audit, 30-day plan, first review. A client who skips the baseline audit churns at roughly twice the rate of one who completes it.

## Role Clarity Framework
A role is defined by four things only: the outcome it owns, the decisions it may make alone, the decisions it must escalate, and the weekly evidence that it is working. Anything else belongs in a process document, not a role document.

## Escalation Ladder
Tier 1 is handled by the coach within 24 hours. Tier 2 goes to the head coach within 48 hours. Tier 3 goes to the founder. A Tier 3 item that sits for more than 72 hours is a systems failure, not a people failure.
