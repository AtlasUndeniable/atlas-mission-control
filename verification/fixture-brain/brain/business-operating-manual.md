# Business Operating Manual

## Client Communication Standards
All client-facing responses go out within one business day. A check-in change request is confirmed in writing with the exact new schedule before it takes effect.

## Coach Responsibilities
The coach owns weekly check-in review, programme adjustment, and flagging retention risk. The coach does not own pricing, contract changes, or refunds.

## Weekly Operating Rhythm
Monday: pipeline and capacity review. Wednesday: client risk review. Friday: delivery audit and handover notes.
