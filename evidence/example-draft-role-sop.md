Client: Northwind Pilates · Workflow: role_sop · Audience: internal_review

## Purpose

Define the coach role that owns the Northwind check-in day change, so the change is confirmed in writing before it starts and the escalation path is unambiguous.

## Source-backed facts

Rhys agreed to write the coach role SOP, including the escalation ladder, before the Northwind check-in change takes effect.
Source: Coach Role SOP — Northwind Check-in Change [commitment]

Northwind Pilates asked to move weekly check-ins from Monday to Thursday because their studio runs its busiest classes on Monday mornings.
Source: Northwind Pilates — Check-in Change Request [asset:client-work:northwind-pilates/check-in-change-brief.md]

All client-facing responses go out within one business day. A check-in change request is confirmed in writing with the exact new schedule before it takes effect.
Source: Business Operating Manual [asset:operating-manual:business-operating-manual.md]

An SOP fails when it describes intent instead of action. "Ensure the client is supported" is not a step. "Send the week-one check-in template by 5pm Monday" is a step.
Source: The Scaling SOPs [asset:lead-magnets:the-scaling-sops-2026.md]

## Proposed procedure — requires human review

1. Confirm the new Thursday check-in day to the client in writing before it takes effect.
   Proposed responsible role: Coach
   Completion check: Written confirmation sent and logged against the client record.
   Exception: If the client does not reply within one business day, escalate to the head coach.

2. Update the check-in schedule in the delivery tracker for the current cycle.
   Proposed responsible role: Unassigned — confirm before use
   Completion check: Tracker shows Thursday for every remaining check-in this cycle.
   Exception: If the tracker cannot be edited, record the change in the handover note and flag it on Friday.

3. Book a review for the end of the following cycle to confirm the new day is working.
   Proposed responsible role: Coach
   Completion check: Review is on the calendar with the client confirmed.
   Exception: If no slot is available, raise it at the Friday delivery audit.

4. Escalate any missed confirmation to the head coach within 48 hours.
   Proposed responsible role: Head coach
   Completion check: Escalation is recorded and an owner is named.
   Exception: If unresolved after 72 hours, treat it as a systems failure and raise it with the founder.
