## Proposed procedure — requires human review

1. Agree the first Thursday on which the new check-in day starts.
   Proposed responsible role: Client principal
   Completion check: A specific date is agreed in writing.
   Exception: If no date is agreed this week, keep Monday check-ins running unchanged.

2. Confirm the change in writing before the first Thursday check-in.
   Proposed responsible role: Coach
   Completion check: Client holds written confirmation of the new day.
   Exception: If confirmation cannot be sent, do not move the check-in.

3. Hold the four monthly check-ins and the monthly strategy call on the revised schedule.
   Proposed responsible role: Coach
   Completion check: All four check-ins and the strategy call are booked on the new day.
   Exception: If a session cannot be rebooked, flag it before the cycle starts rather than dropping it.

4. Review the change at the end of the following cycle, ahead of the quarter-end renewal.
   Proposed responsible role: Coach
   Completion check: Review is complete and the outcome recorded before renewal.
   Exception: If the review slips past renewal, raise it at the Wednesday client risk review.

## Proposed decisions

- Approve the first effective Thursday for the new check-in day.

- Decide whether the monthly strategy call also moves.

## Missing facts
